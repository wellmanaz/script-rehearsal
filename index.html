Script Rehearsal

Run your lines against the rest of the cast. Lines your castmates record play in their voices; anything not recorded is read aloud by your device.

Your scripts

    Scripts and recordings stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to format a script

    Speaker names in capitals work best, in any of these layouts:

    MAYA
You're late.

THEO: I know.

MS. OKAFOR. Places, everyone.

    Scene headings such as SCENE 2, ACT ONE or INT. KITCHEN let you rehearse one scene at a time. Put stage directions in parentheses or on their own lines. Fountain screenplay files work too.

    Who are you playing?
